Slopculator

A calculator and grapher you type into. It runs in your browser and collects nothing.

Open Slopculator Source on GitHub

Free and MIT licensed · {{version}}

In exact mode, 0.1 + 0.2 is 3/10, and 25! keeps all 26 digits.

2x, 3(4 + 5), sin 30 and √16 mean what they look like, and missing brackets get filled in. Ctrl+K finds any function by name or by what it does.

Graphs, statistics, matrices and calculus on one sheet.

Normal, binomial, t, χ² and F distributions, regressions, derivatives, integrals and solve. For HVAC, psychrometrics, 20 refrigerants, ducts and pipes. Every function is in the reference.

No account, analytics, ads or cookies.

Sheets stay in your browser, and the app works offline. You can download it as one HTML file. The source is on GitHub under the MIT license. Read the privacy policy.
