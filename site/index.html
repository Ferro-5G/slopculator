Slopculator

A free, open-source calculator and grapher on a live wired canvas. Runs locally in your browser with zero tracking.

Open Slopculator Source on GitHub
slopculator.com/app

Exact math, natural notation. In exact mode, 0.1 + 0.2 is 3/10 and 25! keeps all 26 digits.

2x, 3(4 + 5), sin 30 and √16 parse as written, brackets auto-close, and Ctrl+K finds any function by name or description.

296 functions on one sheet. Graphing, statistics, matrices, calculus and engineering together.

Normal, binomial, t, χ² and F distributions, regressions, derivatives, integrals and solve — plus HVAC psychrometrics, 20 refrigerants, ducts and pipes. See the function reference.

Local-first and open source. No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.

Your sheets stay in browser storage and the app works offline. You can also download it as a single HTML file. Licensed under MIT; read the privacy policy.
